Frequently Asked Questions

What is Nyaya Sahayak?

Nyaya Sahayak is a legal awareness prototype designed to make basic legal information easier to understand and access.

Can I get free legal assistance?

Eligible people can seek free legal services through Legal Services Authorities. Contact NALSA or your local legal services authority for guidance.

Can I file an official complaint through this website?

No. This student prototype does not currently file complaints with police, courts or government departments.

What should I do if I experience cyber fraud?

Contact 1930 promptly for cyber financial fraud assistance and use the official cybercrime reporting portal.

Is the information on this website legal advice?

No. This website provides general legal awareness and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer.
